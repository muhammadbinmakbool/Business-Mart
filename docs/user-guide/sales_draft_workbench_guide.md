# User Guide: Sales Workbench & Intelligent Draft Invoices

Welcome to the Sales Workbench and Draft Suggestions system guide. This guide explains how to use the automated billing prefill features to speed up your sales entries.

---

## 1. The Sales Workbench (Bulk Prefill Flow)

The **Sales Workbench** aggregates all active buyers who have either:
*   Unbilled intake weights/transactions mapped to them.
*   Recent historical purchase patterns indicating likely repeat orders.

### Step-by-Step Workflow:
1.  Navigate to **Sales Workbench** from the sidebar menu (under the **Sales** dropdown).
2.  Review the cards for each buyer. Each card lists suggested items that are ready to be billed.
3.  **Select Items**: Check or uncheck individual items on the card depending on what you want to bill right now.
4.  **Convert to Invoice**: Click the **Convert** button on the card.
5.  You will be automatically redirected to the **Create Sale** screen.
6.  The items you selected are already prefilled in the products table!
7.  Verify the details, add any adjustments or payments, and click **Save Sale** to finalize.

---

## 2. Direct Sale Prefills (Instant Banner Flow)

If you navigate directly to the standard sales entry screens, you can still access intelligent suggestions on the fly.

### Classic "Create Sale" Form:
1.  Go to **Sales** -> **Create Sale**.
2.  Choose a buyer from the **Select Buyer** dropdown.
3.  If the buyer has active draft suggestions, a banner saying **"Intelligent Draft Suggestion Available"** will pop up immediately.
4.  Click **Use Draft** to prefill the table with all draft items in one click.

### POS Billing Screen:
1.  Go to **Sales** -> **POS Billing**.
2.  Select the buyer in the buyer selector panel.
3.  A compact banner saying **"Intelligent Draft Invoice Available"** will display.
4.  Click **Use Draft** to load the items and continue using fast keyboard shortcuts.
